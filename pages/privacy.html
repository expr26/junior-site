Privacy notice

Short version: children can practise here without an account, we don't use ads or advertising trackers, and we don't collect personal details.

Practising on the site

No account or sign-up is needed. Your child's stars, streak and answers are saved only in this browser on your device (local storage). They are not sent to us. Clearing the browser's site data removes them.

Visitor statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page visits. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't identify individual visitors.

Links to ExaminerPrep

Links to our sister site ExaminerPrep add a tag (utm_source=junior) so it can count visits that came from here. No personal details are passed on. ExaminerPrep has its own privacy notice.

Your rights
