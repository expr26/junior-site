Privacy notice

Short version: children can practise here without an account, we don't use ads or advertising trackers, and we only keep what a parent sends us to arrange lessons.

Practising on the site

No account or sign-up is needed. Your child's stars, streak and answers are saved only in this browser on your device (local storage). They are not sent to us. Clearing the browser's site data removes them.

Visitor statistics

We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page visits. It doesn't use cookies and doesn't identify individual visitors.

The lessons form

When a parent or guardian fills in the lessons form, we keep: your name and email, your child's age and school programme, the subjects, how confident your child feels, what you're looking for, your message, and which site sent you here (for example, a search engine). We don't ask for your child's name.

We use this only to reply to you and arrange lessons. The legal basis is your consent and steps you ask us to take before an agreement (UK and EU GDPR). The data is stored with Cloudflare, and may be emailed to us through our email provider. We delete inquiries that don't lead to lessons within 12 months.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data at any time by writing to {{contact_email}}. You can also complain to your data protection authority (in Greece, the Hellenic Data Protection Authority; in the UK, the ICO).
