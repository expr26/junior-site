Progress report for parents

A summary of your child's practice on this device, made from what is saved in this browser. Nothing is sent to us.

How to read this

Right first time means the answer was right before any hint. Fixed means it was right on the second try, after a hint: that is good learning, not a failure.

A topic appears under worth practising when at least 5 questions were tried and fewer than 6 in 10 were right first time. Short, regular practice helps more than long sessions.

Progress lives only in this browser. Clearing the browser's site data, or using another device, starts from zero. Saved progress across devices is planned with parent accounts.
