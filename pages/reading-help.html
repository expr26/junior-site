Reading help

Every child reads differently. Tap the Aa button at the top of any page to make the site easier to read. Nothing to sign up for, and the choices are saved only in this browser.

What your child can change

  • Letters: Atkinson Hyperlegible and Lexend, which are designed to be easy to read, and OpenDyslexic, which some children with dyslexia prefer.
  • Text size up to one and a half times bigger.
  • Space between lines and space between letters.
  • Page colour: cream, soft blue or plain white instead of the colourful background. Some children find a tinted page more comfortable.
  • Reading ruler: a bright band that follows a finger or the mouse and dims the other lines.
  • Focus mode: hides the owl, background pictures and extras, so only the work is on screen.
  • Calm mode: no confetti and nothing moving on its own. Stars, levels and badges still count. It switches on by itself if your device is set to reduce motion.
  • Read it to me: a button above every question reads the question and its answer choices aloud, with maths symbols said as words (for example “3/4” is read as “3 over 4”). It uses the voice built into your device, so how it sounds depends on the device.

Built in for every child

  • No timers. Questions and games never race the clock.
  • Hints before answers. A wrong first try gets a hint and a second go, then a worked answer.
  • Stars for effort and for fixing mistakes, not for speed, and no leaderboards.
  • Videos with a recorded voice and the words on screen, with a short check after each part.
  • Large buttons for small fingers, keyboard access and clear focus outlines.

A note on the research

Studies have not found that special fonts on their own improve reading for children with dyslexia, but extra letter and line spacing, larger text and hearing the text read aloud help many readers. The best setting is the one your child finds comfortable, so let them try a few.

Privacy

We never ask about a diagnosis or a learning difference. The settings stay in this browser and are not sent to us.
